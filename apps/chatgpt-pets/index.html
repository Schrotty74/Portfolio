What it does

ChatGPT Pets is a collection of custom animated companions that can be imported into ChatGPT and Codex. Each character is presented in its own folder with a preview and an importable sprite-sheet file. The collection currently includes Woodi, WonderCube, Delta, Byte, Whiskers & Squeak, Inky and Finder Guy.

AI-assisted development

Schrotty74 develops these projects with AI assistance. Depending on the project, OpenAI Codex and, in parts, Claude support the development work. Xcode is used for Apple-platform development. Schrotty74 retains final review and responsibility.
