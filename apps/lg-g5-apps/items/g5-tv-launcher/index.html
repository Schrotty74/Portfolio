Project description

A standalone homebrew launcher for a rooted LG G5 with webOS 25. It provides a TV-oriented interface with a customizable favorites dock and input shortcuts.

Features and details

01Browse and launch installed apps from Overview; HDMI inputs and Live TV have their own Inputs page.
02Add, remove and reorder favorites in the glass-style dock. Show app icons with or without names and choose from three icon sizes when names are hidden.
03Navigate using the Magic Remote directional controls, pointer and wheel.
04Open HDMI 1–4 and Live TV. HDMI names can be read from the TV's launch points or customized.
05Choose from five illustrated scenes, shown in the banner on Overview, Favorites and Inputs.
06Show or hide the clock and weather independently, and choose a weather location.
07Select Overview, Favorites or Inputs as the start page, including when reopening the launcher.
08Switch the interface between English and German; English is the default.
09Optionally assign the Home button to this launcher using Magic Mapper 1.1.2, and restore the previous mapping. Requirements and details.
10Save and restore launcher settings, favorites and custom input labels as a JSON backup on the TV.
