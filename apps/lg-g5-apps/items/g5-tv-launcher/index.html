Project description

A standalone homebrew launcher for a rooted LG G5 with webOS 25. It provides a TV-oriented interface with a customizable favorites dock and input shortcuts.

Features and details

01Browse and launch installed apps from Overview; HDMI inputs and Live TV have their own Inputs page.
02Add, remove and reorder favorites in the glass-style dock. Show app icons with or without names and choose from three icon sizes when names are hidden.
03Choose from seven illustrated scenes, shown in the banner on Overview, Favorites and Inputs. Halloween and Christmas are grouped under Special themes.
04With animations enabled, Halloween features a spider descending at changing positions, flickering house windows and a glowing pumpkin face. Christmas features falling snow and Santa's reindeer sleigh flying across the scene in alternating directions. The sleigh flies for 12 seconds with a 5-second pause; the effects can be switched off with animations.
05Show or hide the clock and weather independently, and choose a weather location.
06Select Overview, Favorites or Inputs as the start page, including when reopening the launcher.
07Search installed apps by name.
08Download source ZIP
