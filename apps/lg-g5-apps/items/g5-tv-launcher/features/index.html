G5 TV Launcher

All features

Complete feature list from the current GitHub documentation.

01Browse and launch installed apps from Overview; HDMI inputs and Live TV have their own Inputs page.
02Add, remove and reorder favorites in the glass-style dock. Show app icons with or without names and choose from three icon sizes when names are hidden.
03Navigate using the Magic Remote directional controls, pointer and wheel.
04Open HDMI 1–4 and Live TV. HDMI names can be read from the TV's launch points or customized.
05Choose from seven illustrated scenes, shown in the banner on Overview, Favorites and Inputs. Halloween and Christmas are grouped under Special themes.
06With animations enabled, Halloween features a spider descending at changing positions, flickering house windows and a glowing pumpkin face. Christmas features falling snow and Santa's reindeer sleigh flying across the scene in alternating directions. The sleigh flies for 12 seconds with a 5-second pause; the effects can be switched off with animations.
07Show or hide the clock and weather independently, and choose a weather location.
08Select Overview, Favorites or Inputs as the start page, including when reopening the launcher.
09Switch the interface between English and German; English is the default.
10Optionally assign the Home button to this launcher using Magic Mapper 1.1.2, and restore the previous mapping. Requirements and details.
11Save and restore launcher settings, favorites and custom input labels as a JSON backup on the TV.
12Search installed apps by name.
13Customize the three Overview shortcuts with apps, HDMI inputs or TV settings.
14Rotate backgrounds on each launch or according to the time of day.
15Enable or disable subtle page transitions and magnification of the focused favorite icon.
16Adjust favorite dock spacing, transparency and vertical position.
17Manually check the GitHub source for a newer version; the launcher does not install updates automatically.
18Adjust clock and weather sizes independently; set the weather refresh interval to 15 minutes, hourly, twice daily or daily.
19Show animated weather symbols when animations are enabled, with nighttime, cloudy and rainy variants. Day/night switching uses local sunrise and sunset times.
20Find weather locations in English and German, prefer a match in the TV's time zone, and choose among matching cities with region and country shown.
21Navigate settings by category to reduce scrolling.
