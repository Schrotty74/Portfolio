e2Atlas

Enigma2 player for Mac.

Beta v0.1.0-beta.1

What it does

e2Atlas is a native macOS player for Enigma2 receivers. It connects to compatible receivers through OpenWebif and provides live TV, multiview, channel search, receiver-provided channel logos and a programme guide.

AI-assisted development

Schrotty74 develops these projects with AI assistance. Depending on the project, OpenAI Codex and, in parts, Claude support the development work. Xcode is used for Apple-platform development. Schrotty74 retains final review and responsibility.

Key features

01Watch live TV from an Enigma2 receiver, with bouquets, channel search and receiver-provided logos.
02Follow up to four channels in multiview and save layouts with their audio settings.
03Scroll and search seven days of EPG, with cached listings, recording markers and configurable automatic updates.
04Schedule receiver-side EPGRefresh with a time window, bouquet selection and channel dwell time.
05Schedule and manage receiver recordings from the guide, with conflict details and alternative broadcasts.
06Create AutoTimer rules for recurring title matches and preview matches in the loaded EPG.
07Browse and play receiver recordings, resume saved positions and view receiver storage space.
08Record the original TV stream locally as a .ts file, with folder and duration options and a low-space stop.
09Set programme reminders that macOS can deliver with the app closed, and open programme details from the notification.
10Save favourite channels per receiver and access them independently of the selected bouquet.
