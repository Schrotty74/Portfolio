What it does

CALC BOY is an offline-capable calculator PWA in a classic console style. It combines everyday arithmetic with scientific, programmer, conversion and finance tools, and also includes formula helpers, function plots and small games. The same app can run in a browser or be installed to a phone’s home screen.

AI-assisted development

Developed by Schrotty74 with AI assistance. Depending on the project, OpenAI Codex and, in parts, Claude supported development work; Xcode is used for Apple-platform development. Final review and responsibility remain with Schrotty74.

Complete feature list

01Basic and scientific calculator with trigonometry, logs, powers and memory
02Unit conversion, VAT helpers, savings and interest calculations
03Programmer mode with HEX, BIN, OCT, bitwise operations and RPN stack
04Formula assistant, pixel-style function plots, calculation history and exports
05Seven unlockable console themes, optional 8-bit sounds, Math Attack and Snake
06Calculator history stored on the device
07Installable on a phone home screen
08Offline operation through the service worker
09Reduced-motion support
10English and German interface

Data and privacy

Settings and history are stored only in the browser’s localStorage. The app makes no external requests, uses no analytics and caches only its own files for offline use.

Descriptions, technical information, downloads and manuals are based on the publicly available repository. Links open the selected external service only after you activate them. Where screenshots are displayed, the repository documents them as demo or fictional data.
