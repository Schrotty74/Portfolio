What it does

Apple Shortcuts is a public collection of independently documented Shortcuts for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Every Shortcut lives in its own release folder with instructions, a privacy report, checksums and reproducible download files. The current entry, Weather for City, shows current weather for a saved city in an animated card.

AI-assisted development

Schrotty74 develops these projects with AI assistance. Depending on the project, OpenAI Codex and, in parts, Claude support the development work. Xcode is used for Apple-platform development. Schrotty74 retains final review and responsibility.

Complete feature list

01Per-shortcut releases, documentation, privacy reports and checksums
02Weather for City with a location picker for ambiguous city names
03Saved selected city and a separate Change City action
04Celsius or Fahrenheit based on the city’s country
05Bilingual interface and a built-in update check for the latest release
06Animated weather card with responsive layouts for Mac and iPhone
07Saved city reuse after the first selection
08Separate Change City command
09Location picker for ambiguous cities
